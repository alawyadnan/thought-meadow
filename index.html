Thought Meadow is a simple notes app for iPhone. Capture an idea, plan a lighter day, and keep what matters. No account, ads or tracking.

Submitted to Apple · Waiting for review
Help & feedbackDownload source code

Your thoughts, yours alone

  • Write and save on your device, even offline.
  • Folders, tags, search, Markdown preview and interactive checklists.
  • Optional password encryption, with backups under your control.
  • English and Arabic, with a language choice in Settings.
  • Free and open source under the MIT license.

Sync and shared lists are not available in this release. There are no payments or subscriptions. The app has not yet had an independent security audit.
